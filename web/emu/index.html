HP-1

A handpan for the M-VAVE FM-1, running in your browser from the firmware's own C code

Off. Sound starts when you switch it on.

Playing

  • The white keys are the pan. The two in the middle play the ding, the low center note. The tone fields climb outward from it in a zigzag, left, right, left, right, the way they climb around a real handpan. Every key is in the scale, so anything you play sounds good.
  • Past the pan's own fields, the outermost keys carry the scale on up, like an extended "mutant" pan.
  • The black keys at the ends are the steel: a palm thwap on the outer ones, a fingertip tak on the next ones in, each on its own side of the shell.
  • The seven black keys between are the kit: Gu (a slap on the bottom port), ghost note, ding slap, tak, the ding's harmonic, rim knock, mute. Set Black to Duo (SEL) and they become a second handpan whose scale fits with the first.
  • ARP starts a groove (hold it for its settings). REC mutes. PLAY freezes the grain cloud. OCT− / OCT+ move the pan an octave.

Shaping the instrument

  • SELECT the metal: Nitrided (warm, the classic dark pan), Stainless (bright, the longest ring), Ember (mellow), Raw steel (earthy, drier).
  • ALGORITHM the scale, in the key it's usually built in. PRESETS moves the ding to any root.
  • HOME Pan: metal, strike, sustain, harmonics (how loud the octave and fifth above each note are). Press again for Shell: size (44 to 62 cm), thickness (1.0 to 1.4 mm), sympathy (how much the pan rings along), port (the air inside).
  • ENV Touch: worn, humanize, tuning, release. FX Space, Color. LFO Grain, Spread. SEL Keys, Duo. SEQ Groove. GLO Setup. EDIT steps through them all.
  • Hold SAVE for 1.5 s to go back to the defaults (SAVE again to confirm).

Computer keyboard

  • Space, G, H: the ding. Home row out from the middle: F J D K S L A ;, then R U E I W O.
  • Black keys: 1 to 0 and - (1 and - thwap).
  • B groove, Enter mute, ` freeze, Esc HOME, Z / X octave.
  • ↑ ↓ metal, ← → scale, [ ] key.
  • Mouse and touch: drag a knob up or down, or scroll over it. Shift-click a button to hold it down.

MIDI

Notes strike the note of their pitch at their velocity, so a keyboard or pad controller plays it with real dynamics. Channel 10 plays the steel (kick: Gu, snare: thwap, hats: tak). The sustain pedal holds, the mod wheel sets sympathy, pitch bend bends the ringing notes, program change picks the metal, and MIDI clock leads the groove. Needs Web MIDI (Chrome or Edge).

Install on your FM-1

Beta. Custom firmware is installed at your own risk. Before you start, download M-VAVE's stock firmware (FM-1 V15, "PC Firmware" at m-vave.com/download) so you can always go back (below).

  1. Connect the FM-1 to this computer with a USB data cable, directly (no hub, probe or Transporter). Close other apps that use MIDI.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge, and allow MIDI (with SysEx) when the browser asks.
  3. Press Install. Don't unplug or close this page while it writes (about a minute). The FM-1 restarts into HP-1 and shows its version.

Back to the stock firmware

This writes M-VAVE's own FM-1 V15 back, from HP-1 (or its safe mode, or any Felucca-family firmware) over the same USB cable. Choose the file you downloaded from M-VAVE: it is checked (SHA-256) before anything is written. If the FM-1 doesn't start at all, it comes up in safe mode after two tries, where this still works; past that, tools/fm1_rescue.py in the repository restores it from a Mac.
